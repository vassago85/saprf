## 1. Background and Purpose

The South African Precision Rifle Federation (SAPRF) requires a centralised digital platform to administer memberships, manage match operations, process payments, calculate national and provincial standings, and produce the reports required for federation governance and selection.

This proposal sets out a delivery and sustainability model that allows SAPRF to adopt a fully operational federation platform **without an upfront development fee**. In exchange, SAPRF and Charsley Digital enter into a five-year agreement under which a per-entry platform fee is paid monthly in arrears once the platform is accepted for operational use.

The model is deliberately structured to keep SAPRF's cash exposure low at the outset, while providing Charsley Digital with a predictable revenue stream to fund ongoing hosting, maintenance, and support obligations across the agreement term.

---

## 2. Platform Overview

The SAPRF Federation Platform is a purpose-built web application supporting the federation's two competition disciplines — **PRS (Precision Rifle Series — centrefire)** and **PR22 (rimfire)**.

The platform consolidates the federation's core operational functions into a single system: membership administration, match management, online payments, scoring, national and provincial standings, sponsorship administration, member communication, and management reporting.

The platform is web-based, accessible to members and administrators on desktop and mobile browsers, and is hosted, maintained, and supported by Charsley Digital throughout the agreement term.

## 6. Change Management

This platform is offered to SAPRF at a **substantially reduced commercial model** — there is no upfront development fee, and the monthly platform fee is set at a level that funds ongoing operations rather than open-ended development. For this reason, scope control is essential, and a clear distinction is drawn between activity that is included in the agreement and activity that requires separate approval and quotation.

### 6.1 Included in the Agreement (no additional charge)

- Bug fixes
- Minor operational corrections
- Existing functionality adjustments
- Maintenance activities

### 6.2 Requires Separate Written Approval and Quotation

- New functionality
- Major workflow changes
- Significant UI redesigns
- New integrations
- Additional competition formats
- Any functionality outside the agreed scope

Requests falling under section 6.2 will be assessed by Charsley Digital, quoted separately, and may only proceed once SAPRF has approved the quotation in writing. The existence of pending or future change requests does **not** delay or suspend monthly billing under this agreement.

## 7. Commercial Terms

| Term | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Upfront development fee** | None |
| **Platform fee** | R50 per shooter entry processed through the platform |
| **Annual increase** | In line with the official South African CPI, unless otherwise agreed in writing |
| **Payment terms** | Monthly in arrears |
| **Agreement term** | Five (5) years / sixty (60) consecutive calendar months |
| **Billing commencement** | Immediately upon SAPRF accepting the platform for operational use and the platform becoming the active federation operational system |
| **Billing continuity** | Billing is not delayed or suspended by later change requests, enhancement requests, or additional feature discussions |
| **Payment gateway fees** | Excluded from the platform fee; payable separately |
| **2026-season-onward historical data** | Included at no additional charge |
| **Pre-2026 historical data migration** | Excluded; quoted separately if required |

A "shooter entry" is defined as a single competitor's registration into a single match processed through the platform.

---

## 8. Data Ownership and Continuity

### 8.1 Ownership

SAPRF retains **unrestricted ownership** of all federation data held within the platform, including:

- Membership data
- Match records
- Historical scores
- Financial records
- Federation content

Charsley Digital holds and processes this data on behalf of SAPRF for the duration of the agreement and acknowledges no claim of ownership over any federation data.

### 8.2 Continuity Provisions

If, at any point during the agreement term, Charsley Digital:

- ceases operations,
- discontinues hosting of the platform, or
- becomes unable to fulfil its obligations under this agreement,

then SAPRF will receive, at no additional charge:

- The complete delivered source code
- Database schema documentation
- Configuration documentation
- Deployment documentation
- All federation data
- All relevant system credentials

This handover ensures that SAPRF can continue operating the platform — independently or through an alternative provider — without dependency on the continued operation of Charsley Digital.

---

## 9. Post-Handover Responsibilities

Following any source code and data handover under section 8.2, SAPRF or its appointed provider assumes full responsibility for the platform's ongoing operation, including:

- Hosting
- Security updates
- Framework updates
- Dependency updates
- Database maintenance
- SSL certificates
- Transactional email services
- Bug fixes
- Future compatibility
- Ongoing support

Charsley Digital's obligations under this agreement end upon completion of the handover.

---

## 10. Summary

This proposal provides SAPRF with a fully operational federation management platform under a low-risk, predictable commercial model:

- **No upfront development fee.**
- **A fixed five-year agreement** at R50 per shooter entry, monthly in arrears, CPI-linked annually.
- **Hosting, maintenance, security, support, and transactional email** included for the full term.
- **Defined scope** with clear inclusions and exclusions, and a structured change-management process for anything beyond the agreed scope.
- **Unrestricted SAPRF data ownership** and a clear continuity path that protects the federation in the unlikely event Charsley Digital cannot continue.

The model is designed to give SAPRF immediate operational benefit, controlled and predictable costs, and long-term protection of federation interests.
